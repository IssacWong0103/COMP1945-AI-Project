## 7. Limitations

- **LLM-as-a-judge ground truth:** class and attribute labels, plus outfit/try-on quality, come from independent LLM judges — not human raters. Judge agreement is reported alongside the means.
- **No human study:** nothing here is user satisfaction or usability (RQ7 is out of scope).
- **Still a course-scale set:** 159 photos and 5 wardrobes is Tier 1, not an external benchmark; per-class negative counts remain small.
- **Static data:** one-time retailer snapshot and external API variability limit generalization.
